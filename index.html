FAQ

Questions Moncton Homeowners Ask Us

How much does stump grinding cost in Moncton?

It depends on how big the stump is and how easy it is to get to. Text us a photo and we send you a price the same day. It is free, and the price we say is the price you pay. More than one stump always works out cheaper each.

Are you insured?

Yes. We are insured, so you are covered while we are working in your yard.

Do you clean up the wood chips?

Always. We rake the chips back in the hole and level it off, or we haul them away and leave bare dirt for sod. Your call.

Will the machine wreck my lawn?

No. Our machine runs on rubber tracks that spread the weight out, and it fits through a normal backyard gate. It is easy on your grass going in and out.

How soon can you come out?

You get your price the same day you text us. The grinding itself is usually within a few days, and we tell you the next open day when we send the price.

Can I plant grass or build over the spot after?

Yes. We grind it out deep enough that once you top the hole with soil you can plant grass, lay sod, put in a flower bed, or set a shed or patio right over it.

Do you take down trees too?

No, we stick to stumps. If you need the tree taken down first, call us anyway and we will point you to someone good.

Do you grind stumps in the winter?

Yes, as long as the ground is not frozen hard or under deep snow. Late fall and early spring are our quiet weeks, so it is a good time to book.

What areas around Moncton do you serve?

Moncton, Riverview, Dieppe, Shediac, Salisbury, Petitcodiac, Hillsborough and Berry Mills. If you are nearby, call and ask.

Do I need to be home?

Nope. Lots of our customers come home from work to a flat, clean yard and just e-transfer us after.
